Shadowrocket Setup (iOS)
1
Open Shadowrocket

Tap the + icon in the top right corner of the home screen.

2
Change Type to Subscribe

Tap Type and select Subscribe. (⚠️ Do NOT select Shadowsocks or Clash).

3
Paste the Lite Subscription URL

Paste the copied ZorVPN-lite.txt link into the URL field and name the Remark zorVPN.

4
Set Global Routing to Config

Tap Save. In the bottom bar, select Config routing so domestic apps bypass the VPN.

Scan Subscription QR
Scan with Shadowrocket or FlClash camera

Point your phone's camera or client scanner at the QR code to auto-import.
